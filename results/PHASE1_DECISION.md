# Phase 1 decision

## Decision

**GO for continued analysis, but NO-GO for the original broad “CD33-ASO reverses the AD transcriptome” claim.**

The complete GEO matrices contain 170 samples with all four experimental groups in both hippocampus and frontal cortex. Group sizes are adequate for stratified differential-expression models. The target-engagement result is exceptionally reproducible: CD33 expression is increased in APP/PS1 versus WT and is reduced by CD33-ASO versus control-ASO in both cohorts and both regions.

However, genome-wide reversal is limited. Under a strict probe-level definition requiring both the disease contrast and CD33-specific contrast to pass FDR < 0.05 with opposite directions, only one probe (Cd33 itself) is reproduced across both dose experiments in frontal cortex. No broad, dose-reproducible transcriptome reversal is currently demonstrated.

## Robust findings

1. CD33 target engagement is confirmed in four independent series-by-region strata.
2. Disease-associated CD33 upregulation is strongest and most reproducible in frontal cortex.
3. Frontal-cortex disease effects reproduce substantially better across cohorts than hippocampal effects.
4. A small CD33-ASO response set is reproduced across doses: Cd33, Avil and Fcna in both regions, with additional region-specific genes.
5. The 75 µg/day cohort shows a broader treatment response than the 50 µg/day cohort, consistent with a dose-dependent effect but requiring formal interaction/meta-regression testing.

## Revised publication question

The defensible next question is:

> Which disease-associated and treatment-responsive transcriptional signals are reproducible across dose and brain region after CD33-ASO administration in APP/PS1 mice, and which of these signals translate to human Alzheimer’s disease?

The manuscript should emphasize reproducibility, regional heterogeneity, dose dependence and selective target engagement. It should not claim global reversal unless later pathway-level analyses provide replicated evidence.

## Next analysis gate

Proceed to gene-level probe collapsing, quality-control/PCA, cross-cohort meta-analysis, pathway-level rank enrichment and external human AD validation. Stop the CD33-ASO-centered manuscript if human validation and pathway-level replication both fail; in that case, reuse the clean disease contrasts for a broader APP/PS1-to-human AD translational signature study.
