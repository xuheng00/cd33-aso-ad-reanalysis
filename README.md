# CD33-targeting ASO in APP/PS1 mouse brain: transcriptomic reanalysis

A reproducible reanalysis of two public mouse transcriptomic sub-studies
(GSE74437, GSE74438) examining the effects of a *Cd33*-targeting antisense
oligonucleotide (ASO) in APP/PS1 mice, with external validation in human
Alzheimer's disease (AD) transcriptomic datasets.

Companion manuscript: *Transcriptomic effects of Cd33-targeting antisense
oligonucleotide in APP/PS1 mouse brain: a two-sub-study analysis with human
Alzheimer's disease comparisons*.

## Data sources (all public)

| Dataset | Content | Role |
|---|---|---|
| GSE74437 | APP/PS1 mice, 75 µg/day ASO, frontal cortex + hippocampus | Discovery sub-study 1 |
| GSE74438 | APP/PS1 mice, 50 µg/day ASO, frontal cortex + hippocampus | Discovery sub-study 2 |
| GSE33000 | Human AD prefrontal cortex bulk RNA | Human validation |
| GSE48350 | Human AD brain regions bulk RNA | Human validation |
| GSE5281 | Human AD brain regions bulk RNA | Human validation |
| GSE138852 | Human AD microglia single-nucleus pools | Human validation |
| GSE243292 | Human AD donor-level pathology microglia | Human validation |

Raw data are downloaded directly from GEO. Original experimental details not
documented in the GEO records cannot be independently verified; see the
manuscript Methods.

## Software requirements

- R >= 4.5.0 with packages: limma, ggplot2, patchwork, gridExtra, scales,
  org.Mm.eg.db, clusterProfiler (as used by individual scripts)
- Python >= 3.13 with NumPy and h5py (script 11 only)

## Key results (in `results/`)

- `robust_meta_signals.csv` — gene-level meta-analysis signals (all contrasts)
- `meta_signal_counts.csv` — signal counts by contrast and brain region
- `strict_pathway_reversals.csv` — strict reversal candidates (none in primary analysis)
- `pathway_reversal_all.csv` — full pathway-level test results
- `robust_gene_set_enrichment.csv` — hypergeometric ORA of robust gene sets
  (Supplementary Table S1)
- `cd33_vs_control_overlap.csv` — overlap of CD33-specific vs control-ASO
  robust gene sets
- `mapped_robust_mouse_signatures.csv` — mouse robust signatures mapped to
  human orthologs for validation
- `meta_signature_validation.csv` — human cross-species validation statistics

## Reproducibility note

Scripts read raw series matrices from GEO; intermediate large-scale contrast
tables are regenerated by the scripts and are not committed to this repository.
All analyses used limma linear models with empirical-Bayes moderation and
Benjamini-Hochberg FDR control.
